Field weather laboratory

Snow interrupted by Blizzard

Snow particles coalesce into soft local fields. A blizzard periodically tears across those structures, converts them into fast directional streams, then releases them to re-form into new field bodies.

Intricate coalescence

SNOW seeks compatible neighbours and grows temporary cluster fields. COALESCENCE is shared motion without permanent merging. BLIZZARD interrupts the clusters with a moving pressure front, spin bands, and passing-bit events before the field settles again.
