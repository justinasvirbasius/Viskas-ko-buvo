01 / STANDARD NOTION

Magnitude becomes geometry.

Start simply: φ multiplies displacement. Small values flatten the lattice; larger values amplify peaks, spacing and apparent energy. No mystery is required—only a scalar sampled by a structure.

A literal numerical infinity destroys useful geometry. Here the “∞” regime maps unbounded scalar intent through a stable asymptotic transform. The visible effect keeps intensifying, while the simulation remains finite: cells sharpen, voids deepen, and the field approaches a limiting form.

04 / LAPLACE BETWEEN CHAMBERS

Cells do not merely border. They negotiate.

The next regime treats each Voronoi region as a local scalar chamber. A discrete Laplace pressure compares one chamber against its neighbors. Where the difference is small the membrane relaxes; where it is large a crevice opens, producing ridges, troughs and echo-like separation between two nearby ownership fields.
