Field Strings

Particles claim land through ordinary Voronoi ownership. Development strings bind particles into moving architectures, so whole chains can converge, diverge, or hold formation while the field continues to flow beneath them.

String doctrine

PARTICLE = land seed. STRING = linked development architecture. RELATION = how selected strings treat one another. The cells remain normal Voronoi cells; links and cascades govern motion. Exponents now govern how influence scales: p<1 diffuses a field, p=1 stays linear, and p>1 concentrates influence into sharper local territories.
