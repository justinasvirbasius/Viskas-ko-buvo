Field Strings

Particles claim land through ordinary Voronoi ownership. Development strings bind particles into moving architectures, so whole chains can converge, diverge, or hold formation while the field continues to flow beneath them.

String doctrine

PARTICLE = land seed. STRING = linked development architecture. RELATION = how selected strings treat one another. The cells remain normal Voronoi cells; links and cascades govern motion. EQUAL MASS ≠ EQUAL FIELD: every particle now has the same inertial mass, while phase, polarity, stiffness, spin, and exponent create different field effects. Exponents shape the particle-field law itself, not merely its visible influence. STREAM transfers direction through a passing encounter; COALESCENCE makes compatible particles share motion while INTACT preserves their separate identity and spacing; PASSING BIT flips between 0 and 1 whenever a qualified field-crossing event occurs.
