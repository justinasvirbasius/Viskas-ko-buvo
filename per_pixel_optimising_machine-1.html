Four-pass logic.

The prototype treats rendering as a scarce resource allocation problem rather than a uniform full-screen operation.

01 / SENSE

Measure local energy

Luminance gradients, micro-contrast and synthetic motion build an importance score.

02 / BUDGET

Rank the pixels

The chosen workload budget becomes a percentile threshold over the full raster.

03 / REFINE

Spend where needed

Important regions retain high-frequency structure while low-value regions are cheaply approximated.

04 / RECONSTRUCT

Hide the savings

Neighbour-aware reconstruction keeps the low-budget field continuous instead of blocky.
